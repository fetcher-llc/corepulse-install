- **UPS history charts** were empty: the chart API did not accept the UPS readings. They now draw, and output
  load has its own chart whose scale follows the readings (battery charge keeps 0-100 %).
- **SNMP v1**: one value that never gets an answer no longer fails the whole poll once the device has answered
  others. CyberPower's RMCARD202 stops answering for its uptime about 23 hours after it restarts, which made
  every poll of the UPS time out. A device that stops reporting its uptime now shows "—" instead of an old value.
