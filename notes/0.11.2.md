- A "?" help text near the bottom of the screen or of a box (such as Dismiss ticked under Syslog →
  Anomalies) opens upwards instead of being cut off.
- Discovery → Scan: the address box starts with the addresses of your last scan (scheduled scans do not
  count), and each scan under Recent scans has a Use button that puts its addresses back in the box. A scan
  refused for its addresses keeps what was typed, commas and spaces included.
- Mute rules can also hide their messages on the Syslog page ("Hide it on the Syslog page too"), so a
  device's repeating message no longer buries the others. The messages are still stored and counted; the
  Syslog page says how many are hidden, with a link to show them. Every message on the Syslog page has a Mute
  link (admins) that fills in the device and the text with hiding ticked, and the page has a Muted messages
  button (with how many rules are in force), as the Events page has.
- IoT: "Reached into another network" is now only reported for the networks under Device discovery. Cameras
  and doorbells (Wyze, Ring, Nest, Tuya...) that try the viewing phone's own local address (e.g. 10.121.x.x on
  a mobile network) are a single "Peer-to-peer attempt" information finding: the firewall sent those packets out
  through the internet, or nothing answered. A private address that answered but is in none of your networks is
  reported as "Reached an unknown private address", with a hint to add the network. Contacts are remembered by
  address, so a new random port is not a new finding, and random high ports are no longer shown as the port.
- Flows record whether the firewall translated the sender to a public address (NetFlow/IPFIX NAT fields), so a
  private destination reached through the internet is no longer counted as one of your hosts.
