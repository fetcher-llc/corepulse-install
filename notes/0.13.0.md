- **UPS monitoring**: a UPS with a network card now gets a UPS panel and history on its node page: where its
  power comes from (and for how long it has been on battery), battery charge and health, runtime at the current
  load, load, battery temperature and input/output voltage. CyberPower cards are read through CyberPower's own
  MIB; APC, Eaton, Tripp Lite and Vertiv cards through the standard UPS-MIB. Switching to battery sends an urgent
  ON BATTERY alert and POWER RESTORED when mains returns; low charge, low runtime, overload, a battery that needs
  replacing and a hot battery raise warnings, with the thresholds under Settings → SNMP.
- **SNMP v1** profiles, for old devices that offer nothing newer (CyberPower's RMCARD202, for one). v1 GETs ask
  for one value per request, since such agents fail the whole request, or stop answering, when one value is
  missing; sysObjectID falls back to a GETNEXT for agents that refuse a direct GET of it.
- **SNMP traps**: the community a v1 trap carries (snmpTrapCommunity) is no longer stored in the trap log; it
  is the trap password. CyberPower UPS traps show the card's own description ("UPS: Utility power failure...")
  with a matching severity instead of a bare OID, and "utility power restored" sends a POWER RESTORED alert
  (when recovery alerts are on), so a power blip shorter than the SNMP poll still gets its all-clear. A changed
  trap community now applies within a minute instead of at the receiver's next restart, and Settings → Logs,
  traps & flows has one field per trap community (with "Add another") instead of a comma-separated list.
- Traps sent with a community that is not in the list are no longer lost without a word: Settings → Logs, traps &
  flows lists the devices whose traps were ignored for that reason (address and count; never the community).
- **Test SNMP** now does the same reading as polling, so it passes or fails exactly as polling will, and for a UPS
  it reports the power source, battery, runtime and load.
- **UPS node type**, with its own map icon and setup steps on the node's Set up SNMP page; discovery suggests it
  for UPS makers (CyberPower, APC, Eaton, Tripp Lite).
